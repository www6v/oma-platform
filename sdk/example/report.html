4. The Surprising Pattern: The "Small Category, Big Wallet" Paradox

Office is the second-lowest category by order volume but dominates revenue — and it's entirely concentrated in just three customers.

⚡ The paradox: Office has only 11 orders (22% of volume) yet produces $1,352 in revenue (33.0% of total). Its AOV of $123 is 1.7× Electronics and 2.1× Home.
⚡ Concentration risk: All 11 Office orders come from just 5 customers — and 4 of them are repeat buyers. If any one of these customers churns, Office revenue drops by 20-30%.
